Privacy Policy

Mihon Sync · Last updated October 22, 2026

Mihon Sync ("the app") is an open source comic reader for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The developer does not run any server for the app and does not receive any of your data.

Google account and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional and only needed for syncing. When you sign in, the app asks for:

  • Access to its own app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The app stores one file there: a backup of your library, categories, reading progress, reading history, tracker links, extension repositories and source settings. This folder is hidden and only this app can read it. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email), only to show which account is signed in. It is stored on your device.

This data is sent directly between your device and Google. You can remove it at any time by signing out in the app, by removing the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the app's hidden data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data stored on your device

Your library, reading progress, settings and downloaded chapters are stored on your device. They are not shared with the developer.

Extensions and third-party sites

The app doesn't host any content. Extensions you choose to install connect directly to third-party websites to load comics, and those websites have their own privacy policies. Trackers such as MyAnimeList or AniList are only contacted if you sign in to them.

Analytics

Release builds of Mihon Sync contain no analytics or crash reporting.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the app's source code repository.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/samsulrifai/mihon_sync/issues.
