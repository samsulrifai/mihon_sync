Mihon Sync

A comic reader for Android with Google Drive sync.

Mihon Sync is a fork of the open source reader Mihon. It adds syncing of your library, reading progress, categories and extension repos between your devices, stored in your own Google Drive.

Download the latest release

Features

  • Sign in with Google to keep your library in sync across devices.
  • Automatic sync when the app is opened and left, or on a schedule.
  • Install the extensions your library needs on a new device with one tap.
  • Source icons next to the language badge in the library.
  • Everything else Mihon offers: a configurable reader, trackers, categories, backups and more.
